      How it works

      1. Tell us who you are

        Your name, email, and a password are enough to start.

      2. Choose where to begin

        Start with checking or savings. You get both either way.

      3. Fund it and go

        Make your first deposit, then send, move, and track money right away.

      What happens behind the scenes

      • Your password is never storedWe keep only a salted scrypt hash, so even we can't read it back.
      • Money is counted in whole centsBalances never drift from rounding, and an account can't go below $0.
      • Every move is all or nothingA transfer takes money from one account and adds it to the other in one step, or does neither.
      • Sign-ins expireSessions end after 8 hours or when you sign off, and repeated wrong passwords are slowed down.
